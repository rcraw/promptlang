# Webinar Funnel Strategist Skill
## Complete Marketing Strategy Builder with Multi-Agent Research

### Overview

The **Webinar Funnel Strategist** skill is a comprehensive AI-powered tool that researches markets, analyzes competition, and creates complete webinar funnel strategies ready for implementation. It uses multi-agent swarm intelligence to gather data in parallel and synthesizes findings into actionable blueprints.

**Built by:** humanrace.ai
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Last Updated:** October 2025

---

## What It Does

This skill takes a market segment, business offering, or niche and produces a **complete webinar funnel strategy document** including:

✅ **Market Research** - Audience analysis, pain points, language patterns
✅ **Competitive Intelligence** - Gaps, pricing, what works/doesn't
✅ **Income Opportunities** - Monetization models with benchmarks
✅ **Sentiment Analysis** - Trends, influencers, emerging opportunities
✅ **Audience Segmentation** - Developer vs Executive (or other personas)
✅ **Funnel Best Practices** - 2025 conversion tactics and benchmarks
✅ **Platform Strategy** - Kartra-specific (or alternative) setup
✅ **Ad Strategy** - CPR benchmarks, creative, targeting by platform
✅ **Psychology & Pricing** - Offer design, guarantees, urgency tactics
✅ **Complete Implementation Plan** - 30/60/90 day roadmap

## How It Works

### Phase 1: Multi-Agent Research (30-45 minutes)

The skill spawns **5-9 specialized AI agents** that work in parallel:

1. **Market Research Agent** - Analyzes forums, subreddits, communities
2. **Competitive Intelligence Agent** - Studies courses, webinars, pricing
3. **Income Opportunity Agent** - Finds monetization models and benchmarks
4. **Sentiment Analysis Agent** - Tracks trends, influencers, opportunities
5. **Audience Segmentation Agent** - Defines personas, pain points, behavior
6. **Funnel Best Practices Agent** - Gathers current conversion tactics
7. **Platform Strategy Agent** - Platform-specific setup (Kartra, etc.)
8. **Ad Strategy Agent** - CPR benchmarks, creative templates, targeting
9. **Psychology & Offer Agent** - Pricing psychology, guarantees, urgency

### Phase 2: Strategy Synthesis (15-20 minutes)

A coordinator agent consolidates all research into:
- Market gaps and opportunities
- Optimal positioning and messaging
- Pricing recommendations ($997 vs $8,900 tiers)
- Complete funnel blueprint
- Implementation roadmap

### Phase 3: Deliverable Creation (10-15 minutes)

Produces a comprehensive strategy document with:
- Executive summary
- Market analysis
- Positioning framework
- Complete funnel blueprint (every page, every email)
- Pricing strategy and offer structure
- Ad campaign strategy
- Platform implementation guide
- 30/60/90 day roadmap
- Success metrics and KPIs

**Total Time:** 60-90 minutes for complete strategy

The skill will:
1. Confirm it understands your input
2. Launch research swarm
3. Present preliminary findings
4. Ask clarifying questions if needed
5. Generate complete strategy document

## Limitations & Considerations

### What This Skill Does NOT Do

- ❌ Build the actual pages, funnels, or websites
- ❌ Create the webinar presentation slides
- ❌ Write your entire email sequences word-for-word
- ❌ Manage your ad campaigns
- ❌ Guarantee specific results (market dependent)

### What This Skill DOES Provide

- ✅ Complete strategy and blueprint
- ✅ Templates and frameworks to fill in
- ✅ Specific recommendations with rationale
- ✅ Current market data and benchmarks
- ✅ Step-by-step implementation guide

Think of this skill as your **strategic consultant** - it tells you WHAT to build, WHY it will work, and HOW to implement it. You still need to execute the build.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long does it take to run?
**A:** 60-90 minutes for complete strategy with multi-agent research.

### Q: Can I use this for non-webinar funnels?
**A:** It's optimized for webinar funnels, but the research and positioning work can inform other funnel types.

### Q: Do I need to provide competitor names?
**A:** No, the research agents will find competitors automatically.

### Q: What if my market is very niche?
**A:** That's ideal! The more specific your niche, the better the positioning and messaging recommendations.

### Q: Can this work for B2B and B2C?
**A:** Yes, it adapts recommendations based on your target audience (developers, executives, consumers, etc.).

### Q: How often should I re-run this skill?
**A:** Every 6-12 months, or when entering a new market segment.

### Q: Can I customize the strategy after it's created?
**A:** Absolutely! The strategy is a starting point. Adapt based on your specific needs and test results.

### Q: What if I don't have $5k/month for ads?
**A:** The skill will provide organic alternatives and lower-budget strategies, but webinar funnels typically require paid traffic for scale.
