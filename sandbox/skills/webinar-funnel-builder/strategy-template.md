### 3.4 Webinar Presentation Structure (90 minutes)

**Minutes 0-10: The Big Promise**
- Introduce yourself (authority building)
- State the big promise/transformation
- Share origin story (build likability)
- Set expectations (what we'll cover)

**Minutes 10-70: The Content (Three Secrets Framework)**
- Secret #1: [Core concept] (15-20 min)
- Secret #2: [Core concept] (15-20 min)
- Secret #3: [Core concept] (15-20 min)
- Transition to offer (5-10 min)

**Minutes 70-85: The Stack (Offer Reveal)**
- Introduce program/offer
- Stack the value (component by component)
- Add bonuses (3-5 strategic bonuses)
- Reveal price with anchoring
- Add guarantee (risk reversal)
- Add scarcity (ethical deadline)

**Minutes 85-90: The Close**
- Recap transformation
- Address objections
- Final CTA (multiple times)
- Q&A (sales oriented)
